# Syllink announcement design

## Direction

One focal object, one headline, one update line and one product descriptor. A spacious dark layout echoes the app's floating orb while keeping technical detail in the caption. The blue orb is conceptual promotional artwork, not a claim to be a literal screenshot.

Name: Syllink, from syllables into ink. A web search informed the choice; it is a creative naming proposal and does not assert exclusivity.

## Research

- [Adobe: Common graphic design mistakes and how to avoid them](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/common-graphic-design-mistakes): a quickly understood message, clear hierarchy, restrained fonts and generous spacing.
- [Canva: Design principles](https://www.canva.com/learn/guide-to-understanding-design-principles/): scale and negative space guide attention.
- [Fermion Research: Introducing Phonon 2.5](https://www.fermionresearch.com/research/phonon-2.5): Phonon 2.5 and the English variant, Windows CPU runtime and local serving.
- The current repository audit and runtime verification confirm the app's selected model, offline use after download, live typing and tested recovery changes.

## Generation

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. The original output was copied unmodified into assets/social/syllink-phonon25-launch.png. No third-party product logos or borrowed artwork were used as inputs. The spelling and hierarchy were visually reviewed.

## Final prompt

```text
Use case: ads-marketing.
Create ONE finished premium social-media announcement graphic for a Windows offline voice-dictation app newly named Syllink (syllables into ink), now powered by Phonon 2.5. This is final artwork to share, not a mood board or an app screenshot.
Format: portrait 4:5 aspect ratio, ideally 1600 x 2000 pixels or higher, crisp readable text for a phone feed. Full bleed, no outer mockup frame.

Art direction: exceptionally restrained, polished editorial product-launch design. Near-black ink/charcoal background with almost invisible fine grain. Warm white typography, cool cobalt and a hint of cyan in ONE sculptural glowing organic voice orb. The actual app uses a floating animated orb, so the central visual should feel like that voice presence: a dimensional sphere of softly folded translucent blue silk/light with fluid internal contours, subtle bright edge and controlled soft halo. Rich material and real depth, elegant and calm. Keep the black background clean and flat away from the orb. No nebula, no starfield, no particles, no lens flares, no neon grid. One orb, not multiple decorative blobs. Plenty of intentional empty space; around 45 percent of the composition should breathe.

Composition: strong typographic hierarchy and precise alignment. A small handsome SYLLINK wordmark at the upper-left with generous margins, no complicated logo. Below, a large clean modern neo-grotesk sans-serif headline over two lines, left aligned. The luminous orb sits below the headline, slightly right of center, approximately 28 percent of the canvas width, surrounded by calm negative space. The orb can have one extremely subtle cursor-like vertical line of light within its internal flow, but no extra UI elements. Near the lower-left, show the model announcement in a smaller but clearly legible font. Beneath it, a single subdued descriptor. Align all text to one vertical grid. Use generous safe margins, about 8 percent on all sides. Carefully balance the top headline and lower announcement. Flat straight-on graphic, no angled sheet or device mockup.

Render ONLY these exact words, spelled correctly, with no added words:
1. Small upper-left wordmark: "SYLLINK"
2. Main headline, exactly two lines:
"Your voice."
"Straight to text."
3. Lower announcement: "Now powered by Phonon 2.5"
4. Small supporting line beneath: "Offline dictation for Windows"

The headline must remain the dominant copy; the model announcement is a clear second read. The word Phonon is spelled P-h-o-n-o-n. Do not change Syllink into Sylink. No paragraphs, feature lists, labels, buttons, badges, logos of operating systems, social icons, app windows, microphone clipart, keyboard, laptop, fake metrics, quotation marks around the text, or watermark. High-end, uncluttered and quietly confident; not a generic busy AI technology advertisement.
```
