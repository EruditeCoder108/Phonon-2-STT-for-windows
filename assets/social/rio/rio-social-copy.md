# Rio launch caption

Meet **Rio**, my offline dictation app for Windows. Now powered by **Phonon 2.5 English**.

Press a shortcut, speak, and your words appear as you talk. Recognition runs on your own PC and works offline after the first model download. No API key or cloud transcription needed.

I’ve also been working on Rio’s visual identity: a living glass orb that moves and changes color with your voice.

Still building and refining it. What do you think?
