Mode: built-in image generation. The three studies used the user's screenshot solely as an appearance reference; the poster used the newly generated Liquid Glass study. The video orb is procedural code, with no screenshot texture.

## Common study prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a finished high-resolution square orb concept for a Windows voice-dictation app named Rio. Ideally 2048 x 2048 pixels. The attached screenshot is ONLY an appearance cue: look at the tiny orb at the lower-right to understand its design language, then REBUILD it cleanly with exquisite high-resolution materials. Do not crop, enlarge, copy screenshot pixels, reproduce the UI or include any text. A single centered circular glass shell on a near-black background with generous space. The recognizable structure is a transparent cyan-rimmed sphere with a softly rounded, irregular luminous liquid core suspended inside, and dark breathing space between core and shell. Thin precise rim, tactile glass, carefully shaped inner liquid volume, rich depth, controlled glow. Sharp clean high-resolution rendering with physically plausible reflections; the liquid's gradients may be soft, but the glass rim and silhouette must be exceptionally crisp. No folds of silk, fibers, vortex, wireframe, microphone, cursor, icons, extra spheres, borders, lettering or watermark. This is a new material study, not a screenshot edit.

Each study appended its respective paragraph:

Variation C — AURORA. Explore the app's changing color as someone speaks.
